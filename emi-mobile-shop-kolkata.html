Frequently Asked Questions

Which documents do I need for mobile EMI at Arnav Telecom?

For most finance partners you only need your Aadhaar card and PAN card. Depending on the partner and amount, a bank statement may occasionally be requested. Approval typically happens in minutes, in-store.

Can I get phone EMI without a credit card?

Yes — that is our speciality. Bajaj Finserv, Home Credit, HDB Financial, IDFC First Bank and PayTm Postpaid all offer cardless EMI at our stores. Debit-card EMI is also available on eligible cards.

Is zero down payment really available?

On many models, yes. Zero or low down-payment schemes depend on the model and the finance partner's current offer — message us on WhatsApp with the phone you want and we will tell you the exact scheme today.

Do all 7 Arnav Telecom stores offer EMI?

Yes. Every store — Garia, Mi Store Garia, Rajpur Sonarpur, Sonarpur Station, Natunhat Boral, Boral Rakshiter More, and Bandel Hooghly — has in-store finance desks for instant processing.
