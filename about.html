Our Story

Redefining electronics retail in Bengal since 2014.

Trust Built Over Years

Established in 2014, Arnav Telecom began with a simple mission: to make premium technology accessible and convenient for the people of Kolkata and Hooghly.

We noticed that buying a smartphone or laptop often meant spending hours in crowded markets, haggling, and compromising on service. We wanted to change that.

Our Mission
