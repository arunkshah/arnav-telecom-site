iPhone vs Samsung: The Ultimate Showdown

May 8, 2026 Arnav Telecom

The Apple vs Samsung debate never goes away, and for good reason — both brands make outstanding flagship phones with very different philosophies. At Arnav Telecom, our sales executives are asked this question dozens of times every day. So we've put together the most thorough comparison we can to help you decide: the iPhone 15 Pro vs the Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra.

Price & EMI Availability in Kolkata

The iPhone 15 Pro starts at around ₹1,29,900, while the Galaxy S24 Ultra starts at ₹1,29,999. Both are available at Arnav Telecom with No Cost EMI starting at ₹3,999/month on a 36-month tenure through Bajaj Finserv or HDFC Bank. Our team can also arrange doorstep demo so you can try both before committing.

Display & Design

The iPhone 15 Pro features a 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR ProMotion display (120Hz) with a titanium frame — one of the lightest premium smartphones ever. Samsung's S24 Ultra has a larger 6.8-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED display, also at 120Hz but with higher brightness and the built-in S-Pen stylus. If you prefer a compact phone, go iPhone. If you want a large canvas for productivity, go Samsung.

Camera System

This is where things get interesting. Apple's iPhone 15 Pro uses a 48 MP main sensor with a new 3x tetraprism optical zoom. The video quality — especially in ProRes and Log formats — is unmatched for content creators. Samsung's S24 Ultra has a 200 MP main sensor and a 10x optical zoom, making it the zoom king. For still photography in bright light, the S24 Ultra wins. For video and consistent colour science, the iPhone wins.

Performance & Software

The iPhone 15 Pro runs on Apple's A17 Pro chip, the most powerful mobile processor in the world. It handles everything from casual use to heavy 4K video editing without breaking a sweat. The Galaxy S24 Ultra runs on Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 for Galaxy — which is genuinely faster than any previous Samsung flagship and introduces Galaxy AI features like live translation, chat assist, and circle-to-search.

Software support is a key differentiator: Apple guarantees 6 years of iOS updates for the iPhone 15 Pro. Samsung now promises 7 years of Android OS and security updates for the S24 Ultra — an industry first for Android.

Ecosystem & Connectivity

If you use a MacBook, iPad, AirPods, or Apple Watch, the iPhone integrates seamlessly through AirDrop, Handoff, and iMessage. Samsung integrates with Windows PCs through the Link to Windows app and with Galaxy Watch, Galaxy Buds, and Galaxy Tab devices. If your family already uses iPhones, switching to Android can disrupt iMessage group chats and FaceTime calls.

The Verdict for Kolkata Buyers

There is no single right answer, but here's a simple guide:

  • Choose iPhone 15 Pro if you value software polish, video quality, ecosystem integration, and a compact premium design.
  • Choose Galaxy S24 Ultra if you want the largest display, best still camera zoom, S-Pen productivity, and Galaxy AI features.
