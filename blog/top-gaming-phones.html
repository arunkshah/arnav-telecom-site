What Makes a True Gaming Phone?

Three things separate a gaming phone from a regular smartphone:

  • Raw Performance: Snapdragon 8-series or equivalent chip with a high-speed LPDDR5X RAM and UFS 4.0 storage for near-instant app loads.
  • Thermal Management: Vapour chambers and graphite cooling to sustain peak performance for hours without throttling.
  • Display Responsiveness: 144Hz refresh rate and high touch sampling rate (360Hz+) for smoother, more responsive controls.
