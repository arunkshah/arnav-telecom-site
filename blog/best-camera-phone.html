Best Camera Phones of 2026

May 5, 2026 Arnav Telecom

Whether you're a content creator, travel photographer, or just want the best family photos, the camera is the single most-discussed feature when customers walk into Arnav Telecom. In 2026, camera technology has leaped forward dramatically. Here are the top picks our expert staff recommend across all budget ranges.

What Makes a Great Camera Phone in 2026?

Three things define a great camera phone: sensor size (bigger = better low-light performance), computational photography (how the software processes the image), and optical zoom (how close you can get without losing quality). All phones on this list excel in at least two of these three areas.

Top 5 Camera Phones of 2026 Available at Arnav Telecom

1. iPhone 15 Pro Max — Best for Video & Colour Accuracy

The iPhone 15 Pro Max is still the gold standard for video recording in 2026. Its new 5x Tetraprism optical zoom gives you incredible reach without digital quality loss, and the Apple ProRes Log format gives videographers raw footage that's a joy to colour-grade. The natural, consistent colour science Apple uses means your photos look true-to-life without heavy processing. At Arnav Telecom, the iPhone 15 Pro Max is available with No Cost EMI from ₹4,499/month.

Key specs: 48 MP main | 12 MP ultrawide | 12 MP 5x periscope | 4K ProRes video

2. Vivo X100 Pro — Best Portrait Camera

Co-engineered with ZEISS, the Vivo X100 Pro takes the best portrait photos we have ever seen on a smartphone at any price. The ZEISS-tuned 50 MP main sensor, combined with a 50 MP periscope telephoto, produces lens-flare effects and bokeh that look genuinely cinematic. Night photography is also exceptional thanks to the large sensor and ZEISS T* anti-reflective coating. This phone is a must-see in our Garia and Rajpur stores.

Key specs: 50 MP ZEISS | 50 MP periscope | 4K video | Dimensity 9300 chip

3. Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra — Best Zoom Camera

For sheer camera versatility, nothing beats the Galaxy S24 Ultra. Its 200 MP main sensor captures incredible detail, but the standout feature is the 10x optical zoom that lets you photograph distant subjects with crystal clarity. The quad-camera setup — ultra-wide, main, 3x zoom, 10x zoom — means you always have the right lens for any moment. Galaxy AI's photo editing features (like Generative Edit to remove objects) are also genuinely useful.

Key specs: 200 MP main | 50 MP 5x | 10 MP 10x | 12 MP ultrawide

4. Google Pixel 8 Pro — Best AI Camera

Google's Pixel 8 Pro may not have the most megapixels, but its AI-powered camera software is unmatched. Features like Best Take (combines the best expressions from multiple group shots), Magic Eraser (remove unwanted objects), and Photo Unblur work with a reliability no other phone can match. If you want a phone that makes your photos look great automatically — without any editing skills — this is it.

Key specs: 50 MP main | 48 MP telephoto | 10.5 MP ultrawide | Tensor G3 chip

5. OnePlus 12 — Best Budget Camera (Under ₹65,000)

The OnePlus 12 is the most affordable phone on this list and yet it holds its own impressively. Hasselblad-tuned cameras with a 50 MP Periscope telephoto and natural colour calibration make it a serious photography tool. The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 also ensures 4K video is silky smooth. Available at Arnav Telecom with easy EMI from ₹1,999/month.

Our Expert Recommendation

If budget is not a concern and you shoot video: iPhone 15 Pro Max. For portraits and Instagram: Vivo X100 Pro. For versatility and zoom: Samsung S24 Ultra. For AI magic: Pixel 8 Pro. For value: OnePlus 12.
